# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 2, 2026**

## Introduction

Lista de Compras ("the App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your information.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Lista de Compras application.

Lista de Compras is provided free of charge and is intended to be used as provided.

The App allows you to create and manage shopping lists, create and save recipes, and export recipe ingredients to shopping lists. The App is designed to allow you to access your saved information without requiring an Internet connection.

By using the App, you acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

---

## Information Storage

Lista de Compras is designed to store information generated through your use of the App locally on your device.

Depending on the features you use, information stored locally may include:

* Shopping lists
* Items added to shopping lists
* Recipes that you create
* Recipe ingredients
* Recipe preparation instructions
* Other information that you choose to enter or generate while using the App
* App preferences and settings

Your shopping lists, recipes, and other information entered into the App are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to or stored on servers operated by the developer.

The App is designed to allow you to access your saved lists and recipes without requiring an Internet connection.

No user account or registration is required to use the App.

The developer does not have direct access to information stored locally within the App.

---

## Personal Information

Lista de Compras does not require you to provide personal information such as:

* Name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Home address
* Government identification
* Photos

The App does not require an account to access its core features.

Shopping lists, recipes, and other information that you enter or generate while using the App are stored locally on your device.

The developer does not independently collect or maintain this information on its own servers.

## Shopping Lists and User-Generated Content

Lista de Compras allows you to create, edit, organize, and manage shopping lists on your device.

User-generated content may include:

* Shopping list names
* Shopping items
* Item quantities or descriptions
* Recipes
* Recipe names
* Ingredients
* Preparation instructions
* Other information voluntarily entered into the App

The App may also allow you to export ingredients from recipes to a shopping list.

This information is used solely to provide the App's shopping list and recipe-management functionality.

Shopping lists, recipes, and other user-generated content are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to or maintained on servers operated by the developer.

If the App is uninstalled or its local storage is cleared, locally stored lists, recipes, and related information may be deleted.

## Notifications

Notifications are used to provide functionality requested or configured by the user within the App.

The App does not require you to provide personal information in order to use its notification functionality.

## Data Deletion

Lista de Compras stores user-generated information, including shopping lists, recipes, ingredients, and related information, locally on the user's device.

Because the developer does not maintain a server-side account or database containing this information, the developer cannot directly delete information stored locally on your device.

If you wish to delete the information stored by Lista de Compras, you can:

* Use any data-clearing or deletion functionality provided within the App, if available;
* Delete individual shopping lists, items, recipes, or other content through the App, if these functionalities are available;
* Clear the App's storage/data through your Android device settings; or
* Uninstall Lista de Compras from your device.

These actions remove locally stored App data associated with the installation, depending on the action performed.

Please note that information processed independently by third-party services, such as Google AdMob, is subject to the respective provider's privacy policies and available privacy controls.

If you have questions about information processed by a third-party service, you should contact the relevant service provider directly.
